01 / About

A little about
how I think.

Innovation is more than having an idea. It is about understanding a problem, exploring possibilities and communicating a solution clearly.

I'm a student with interests in innovation, creative problem-solving, research and design. Through collaborative projects and four years of exposure to the Atal Tinkering Lab, I've developed an interest in how ideas move from early concepts towards practical models.

I enjoy taking responsibility in team projects, especially in research and design. I value curiosity, clear communication and the willingness to look at a challenge from a different perspective.
